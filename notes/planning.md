# Personal assistant planning — 2026-11-09

**Status:** Discussion only. Default graph memory covers Victor conversations. Extracted facts enter the graph immediately and remain until corrected or deleted; full source conversations remain until deletion. Victor accepts actual passwords in ordinary chat, retains the protected full value in the source message and graph, and automatically detects them without a required secret-entry command. This detection can miss unmarked passwords and remains a material security risk. The app will be privately installed on potentially multiple recent Android phones. Auto Record is a separate user-enabled ambient microphone feature with post-processing, expected to run under one hour/day total across phones. Speech and meetings receive transcription priority; TV is processed on request. It would be used in India without informing other people; the user reports explicit company permission to record work meetings and upload audio/transcripts to personal AWS. At the $50/month app processing limit, audio waits in a queue for user selection; unprocessed audio expires seven days after capture. Ambient findings stay outside the personal graph unless manually saved. Analyzed raw audio is deleted after seven days; transcripts and summaries remain until user deletion. Personal data and recordings should reside in AWS Asia Pacific (Mumbai), `ap-south-1`. English is the initial transcription language. Account numbers, financial credentials, and other personal passwords require passkey access; other financial facts do not by default. Confidential retrieval uses passkey/device verification plus a server check, valid for 15 minutes; Victor may speak a password or other confidential content only when explicitly asked within that window. Google Assistant launch is acceptable for v1. Company Gmail use is reported permitted; only sender, subject, date, short summary, and action items are retained for 90 days, without full bodies or attachments. Exact devices and other design choices remain open. No implementation task, AWS deployment, or Gmail connection is approved yet. Related ideas: IDEA-001 through IDEA-007 in `notes/ideas.md`.

## Working architecture to evaluate

1. **Android:** privately installed text chat, push-to-talk, playback, explicit Personal/English Practice mode, and an Auto Record control. Each enrolled phone has its own device ID and upload queue. Encrypt queued recordings on device and let the user pause or delete them.
2. **API and identity:** Amazon Cognito for sign-in; API Gateway and Lambda for authenticated requests. For confidential retrieval, use a fresh passkey assertion unlocked by the phone's biometric or screen lock and verified by the server. The backend, not the Android interface or LLM, enforces access. [AWS mobile backend](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/whitepapers/latest/serverless-multi-tier-architectures-api-gateway-lambda/mobile-backend.html), [Cognito step-up](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cognito/latest/developerguide/cognito-user-pools-step-up-authentication.html), [passkey server verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/passkeys/developer-guides/server-authentication).
3. **Memory:** store Victor conversations and timestamps separately from extracted graph facts. An extraction workflow proposes entities and relationships with provenance, confidence, sensitivity, and revision history. The user can correct and delete them. Outside conversations do not enter this graph by default. DynamoDB supports adjacency-list graph modeling; consider Neptune only if real multi-hop queries justify it. [AWS DynamoDB graph modeling](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/bp-adjacency-graphs.html).
4. **Voice and files:** evaluate Amazon Transcribe for speech-to-text, a chosen model through Amazon Bedrock for responses/extraction, and S3 for opted-in audio. Use asynchronous batch processing for ambient recordings. [Amazon Transcribe batch](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transcribe/latest/dg/what-is.html), [AWS serverless service guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/decision-guides/latest/decision-guides/choosing-aws-serverless-service.html).
5. **Isolation:** English Practice gets a separate data namespace and backend access path; no automatic write to or retrieval from personal memory. Personal and company Gmail get separate connections and storage. A classifier may suggest mode changes but must not be the only boundary.

| Ambient recording | Android requires microphone permission and a microphone foreground service for ongoing background capture. Starting such a service from the background is restricted; on Android 14+, a microphone service cannot restart directly from boot. Capture may pause after reboot, app termination, permission revocation, or competing microphone use. Android provides capture indicators; a truly invisible recorder is not a dependable design. Show when capture is interrupted. Do not implement indicator-hiding or covert restart behavior. |
| “Every conversation” | Clarified: this means conversations with Victor for automatic graph memory. Auto Record is separately intended to capture whatever the microphone hears while active, including meetings and TV. It cannot guarantee complete capture or direct call/other-app audio. [Android audio input sharing](https://developer.android.com/media/platform/sharing-audio-input) | Capture Victor chat and voice sessions in the graph. Keep ambient post-processing in a separate store unless the user chooses another rule. |

Android's selected `VoiceInteractionService` can be kept running for hotwords; viability depends on the device and selected assistant setup. | Confidentiality | Account numbers, financial credentials, and other personal passwords require step-up. Other financial facts do not by default. The LLM might expose a secret through direct answers, summaries, search results, or speech if access control is only prompt-based. A local biometric success alone is insufficient for the server. [Cognito step-up](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cognito/latest/developerguide/cognito-user-pools-step-up-authentication.html), [passkey verification](https://developers.google.com/identity/passkeys/developer-guides/server-authentication) | Require a server-verified passkey check valid for 15 minutes on this device/session and recheck authorization on each retrieval. Speak confidential content, including passwords, only after authentication and an explicit request within the window. For passwords, use an offline on-device TTS voice; if none is available, show the value privately instead. [Android voice network requirement](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/speech/tts/Voice). |
| Password values in chat | The user chose automatic detection in any chat message and wants the full password value retained behind passkey access. Classification can miss arbitrary values; AWS describes sensitive-information filters as probabilistic, and model invocation logging can contain prompts/responses if enabled. A missed value could reach the LLM or be stored as an ordinary fact. A separate AWS Secrets Manager item for every password has a per-secret monthly cost under the $50 budget. [Bedrock sensitive filters](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/guardrails-sensitive-filters.html), [Bedrock invocation logging](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/model-invocation-logging.html), [Secrets Manager pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/secrets-manager/pricing/) | Run local/backend pre-model detection and pause uncertain messages for user confirmation. Encrypt detected secret spans in source messages and graph properties, mask them in ordinary views/search, route retrieval around the LLM, keep secret values out of application/model logs, and allow correction of missed classifications. Confident false negatives remain possible. Use attribute-level encryption or an equivalently isolated vault and review its cost. [AWS Database Encryption SDK](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/database-encryption-sdk/latest/devguide/dynamodb-encryption-client.html). |

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act's principal processing provisions are scheduled to commence 18 months after 13 November 2025, so its personal/domestic exception is not a current blanket answer. [Supreme Court judgment](https://www.api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2021/31421/31421_2021_5_1501_62289_Judgement_14-Jul-2025.pdf), [DPDP commencement notification](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/267647.pdf), [DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf) | Preserve the scope/conditions of company permission in the design and verify applicable rules for personal settings. Keep a visible recording state and user control. |
| Memory quality | Speech recognition and LLM extraction can mishear, invent, or overstate facts; “store every detail” also raises deletion and retention questions. | Keep raw source references, confidence, contradictory facts, correction history, and a review/delete interface. Separate assertions from verified facts. |

## Auto Record post-processing proposal

1. **Capture:** After the user enables Auto Record, a foreground microphone service records timestamped, encrypted chunks on each phone. The app shows the active state and records gaps if capture is interrupted. A phone cannot resume microphone capture silently after reboot on recent Android. [Android boot restriction](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/changes/foreground-service-types).
2. **Transfer:** Each chunk has device/session IDs, start/end times, checksum, and retry state. Upload to S3 over TLS when network/battery rules allow. Make retries idempotent per chunk and flag overlapping recordings from different phones; those recordings will not have identical checksums. [S3 presigned uploads](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/PresignedUrlUploadObject.html), [Android WorkManager constraints](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/background-tasks/optimize-battery).
3. **Queue and budget gate:** A server-side queue tracks pending minutes and estimated spend across all phones. Apply an app-level $50/month processing decision limit before invoking Transcribe, with headroom reserved for other AWS services. At the limit, keep unprocessed audio visible and ask the user which recordings to process. Delete any recording still unprocessed seven days after capture, even if the next month's allowance has not started; warn before expiry. AWS budget alerts are delayed and are not a guaranteed bill cap. [AWS budget timing](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html).
4. **Analyze:** Prioritize speech and meetings for batch transcription; process TV audio only on explicit request. Identifying TV versus live speech before transcription is imperfect, so show a category and let the user correct it. Optionally label speakers, extract a summary and action items, and attach source timestamps/confidence. Speaker labels do not identify a person across recordings, and overlapping or distant speech can reduce accuracy. [Amazon Transcribe speaker diarization](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transcribe/latest/dg/diarization.html), [Transcribe limitations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/transcribe-speech-recognition/).
5. **Review and retain:** Keep ambient findings separate from Victor's personal graph unless the user manually saves a finding. Delete raw ambient audio seven days after analysis, including cloud and local copies; delete audio never analyzed seven days after capture. Keep transcripts and summaries until the user deletes them, with clear delete/export controls. Define failed-job handling separately.
